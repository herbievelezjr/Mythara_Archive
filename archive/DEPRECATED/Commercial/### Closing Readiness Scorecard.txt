### Scoring Legend

- **Ready** — Artifact present, signed (where applicable), and verified against checksums. No blockers.  
- **Partial** — Artifact exists but requires completion, signature, or minor remediation. Closing allowed with conditioned acceptance.  
- **Not Ready** — Blocker that must be resolved prior to escrow release or final payment.

---

### Top 6 Closing Blockers and Remediations

1. Prebuilt Container Artifact Missing  
   - Action: Decide whether to include demo_container.tar.gz. If included, produce, checksum, and sign; otherwise update escrow release clause to permit rebuild only. Owner: DevOps. Timeline: 48–72 hours.

2. Escrow Agent Contract Not Executed  
   - Action: Select escrow provider, finalize SOW for holdings and triggers, execute escrow contract. Owner: Legal/Finance. Timeline: 5–10 business days.

3. Legal Signatures on Commercial Documents  
   - Action: Collect final signatures on MSA, NDA, and SOW; attach signed copies to manifest/provenance. Owner: Legal. Timeline: 3–5 business days.

4. Banking Details Not Finalized for Remittance  
   - Action: Insert verified account details into remittance_block.txt and provide secure transmission method to payers. Owner: Finance. Timeline: 1–3 business days.

5. Operational Onboarding Runbook Incomplete  
   - Action: Produce onboarding runbook for licensee operations, include rollback and escalation paths. Owner: Ops. Timeline: 3–7 days.

6. Contributor Assignments Require Legal Clearance  
   - Action: Complete signed IP transfer forms, escrow originals, and record redaction release conditions. Owner: Legal. Timeline: 5–7 days.

---

### Acceptance Criteria for Closing

- All **Ready** items remain verified and checksums unchanged.  
- All **Partial** items either completed or accepted with documented mitigations and conditional release terms recorded in SOW.  
- All **Not Ready** items resolved or explicitly moved into escrow unlocking clauses with mutually agreed triggers.  
- Escrow contract executed and escrow agent has verified receipt of signed artifacts and checksums.  
- Finance has a confirmed remittance path and payment milestones agreed in SOW.
