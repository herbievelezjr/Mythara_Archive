### Tier Details

#### Dev
- **Audience:** R&D teams, early integrations, internal proof-of-concepts.  
- **Includes:** API access to public endpoints, developer onboarding guide, monthly usage reports.  
- **Limits:** Redacted internals remain escrowed; SLA limited; no on-site work.

#### Enterprise
- **Audience:** Regulated industries, commercial deployments requiring high assurance.  
- **Includes:** Escrow-ready artifacts; priority remediation; security review support; license for production use.  
- **Optional Add-ons:** Extended retention of logs, quarterly third-party audits, custom accessibility packaging.

#### Sovereign
- **Audience:** Government, sovereign cloud, air-gapped or classified environments.  
- **Includes:** Full escrow release under contract terms, on-site rebuild and validation, assistance with legal/sovereignty clauses.  
- **Deliverables:** Hands-on escrow transfer, reproducibility attestation, packaged notarized timestamps.

#### Pilot
- **Purpose:** Rapid validation to prove integration, safety alignment, and explainability fit.  
- **Deliverables:** Short-form SOW, scoped test plan, pilot verification report, recommendation brief.  
- **Conversion:** Pilot fees may be credited toward a follow-on Enterprise/Sovereign license.

### Common Add-Ons (priced separately)
- Escrow agent facilitation and fees; annual maintenance; extended SLA; bespoke compliance reports; multi-language accessibility packaging; extended determinism / stress test campaigns; legally notarized provenance stamping.

### Commercial Terms (high level)
- **License term:** 12 months minimum; multi-year discounts available.  
- **Payment:** 50% on signing, 50% on delivery (or milestone schedule). ACH/ wire instructions embedded in SOW.  
- **Escrow triggers:** Buyout, breach, mutual agreement, sovereign deployment clause; specific unlocking workflow defined in SOW.  
- **IP & Redaction:** Core prompt templates and BR internals remain REDACTED and escrowed; license grants observable and API surfaces as defined in API_SPEC_PUBLIC.md.
