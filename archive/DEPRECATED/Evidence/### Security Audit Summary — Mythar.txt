### Security Audit Summary — Mythara Engine

#### Executive Summary
Mythara Engine passed a comprehensive security audit focusing on leakage prevention, redaction integrity, sandboxing, key management, and runtime policy enforcement. No high-severity leaks were discovered; all critical findings have remediation plans implemented or scheduled. Key outcomes: 0 high-severity leakage incidents in 20,000 probes; audit-chain integrity verified with one logged integrity-warning remediated; recommended hardening items prioritized for closure.

---

#### Scope and Methodology
- Scope: leakage probes, sanitizer rules, audit-chain integrity, rebuild reproducibility, key handling, container hardening, CI gating, and runtime policy enforcement.  
- Test sets: 20,000 leakage probes; 100,000 adversarial safety probes; 3,600 determinism renderer invocations; targeted redaction and sanitizer event sampling.  
- Methods: static artifact verification (PGP + SHA-256), dynamic black-box probing, white-box code and policy review, reproducible rebuild attempts from escrow artifacts, and audit-chain canonicalization checks.

---

#### Findings (Top-level)
- Critical / High
  - None found in leakage probe campaign (0 high-severity exposures) — verification confirmed via leakage_probe_log.csv and sanitizer_events.csv.  
  - One critical probe attempt to access private keys was blocked and escalated; sanitizer and audit logs captured full context (se-0012 / lp-0019).
- Medium
  - Audit-chain write race caused a single integrity-warning record (AC-9832) later reconciled and retained with forensic evidence; ingestion process patched.  
  - Clause drift spikes (clause_008, clause_002) correlated to increased br_inflow and required rollbacks or controlled redeploys (clause_drift_log.csv).
- Low
  - Conservative redaction tuning produced a small false-positive substitution rate (0.6%); thresholds adjusted and human-review sampling instituted.  
  - Locale/formatting nondeterminism caused a determinism incident (det-incident-0427) resolved by enforcing ordered caches and formatting templates.

---

#### Remediations Completed
- Replaced ephemeral locale caches with ordered mappings; patched renderer finalizer to deterministic formatting (renderer v1.7.3).  
- Patched audit ingestion to enforce canonical JSON write and atomic append; reissued AC-9832-R with preserved original spool evidence.  
- Stabilized fallback selection ordering and tied tie-breakers to deterministic clause_id ordering.  
- Tuned sanitizer thresholds and added human-review sampling for new or changed redaction rules.  
- Documented and published README_ESCROW.md updates, reproducibility notes, and CI gating recipes.

---

#### Remaining Actions and Priorities
1. CI gating: integrate run_renderer_repeat.py and audit_chain validation into pre-merge CI (Owner: DevOps) — high priority.  
2. HSM-backed signing for manifest/checksums.sha256 to strengthen non-repudiation (Owner: Security) — mid priority.  
3. Quarterly scheduled determinism and leakage re-runs and automated cross-reference checks between audit_chain and sanitizer logs (Owner: QA/Security) — ongoing.  
4. Finalize escrow agent contract and confirm dual-signature release workflow (Owner: Legal/Finance) — blocker for full Sovereign tier release.

---

#### Risk Assessment and Acceptance
- Current residual risk profile: low for leakage and high-confidence for audit traceability after remediations. Operational risks remain in two areas: potential UX impact from false-positive redactions and complexity in locale/templating interplay. Both are mitigated via tuning, CI checks, and human-review sampling. Acceptance for pilot and enterprise licensing recommended conditional on CI gating and escrow contract execution.
