```text
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of [Date] between:
Assignor: [Collaborator Full Name], Address: [Address]
Assignee: Mythara Labs LLC, Address: [Address]

1. Assignment. Assignor hereby assigns to Assignee all right, title and interest in and to all intellectual property, including but not limited to copyrights, inventions, designs, know‑how, trade secrets, and moral rights, created, developed, or contributed by Assignor, whether alone or with others, relating to the project identified as "Mythara Engine" and manifest reference ME-archive-0001 (the "Work"), during the period [start date] to [end date or "ongoing"].

2. Consideration. In consideration for this assignment, Assignee shall provide [describe consideration: payment, equity, credit, etc.], receipt of which Assignor hereby acknowledges.

3. Waiver of Moral Rights. To the extent permitted by law, Assignor waives any moral rights in the Work and grants Assignee the right to alter, adapt, publish, or otherwise exploit the Work.

4. Representations and Warranties. Assignor represents that (a) the Work is original to Assignor or properly licensed to Assignor, (b) Assignor has full authority to assign the Work, and (c) assignment does not violate rights of third parties.

5. Further Assurances. Assignor agrees to execute documents and perform acts reasonably required to effectuate this assignment (including assignment affidavits and inventor’s declarations).

6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State/Country]. Venue for disputes: [county/state].

7. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
```

### Operational checklist to collect valid signatures
- Decide signature method: (A) in-person notarized wet signatures, or (B) reputable e-sign (DocuSign, Adobe Sign, HelloSign). Prefer HSM/e‑signature provider with audit trail.
- Prepare one executed copy per party; store master in encrypted, access‑controlled repository.
- Require government ID check for external collaborators when notarizing or using e-sign KYC.
- Record metadata: timestamp (UTC), signer IP (for remote e-sign), signer email, signer verification method, document hash (sha256) and detached signature if available.
- Canonicalize and record: before signing, canonicalize final PDF (flatten fields), compute SHA-256, and record hash in RELEASE_MANIFEST.json and escrow bundle.
- Notarization: if notarized, capture notarization affidavit, notary seal image, and notary commission details; store signed PDF and notarization metadata.
- Witnessing: if witness signatures used, capture witness name, contact, and relationship to signer.
- Retention: store original signed documents in write-once or immutability-tier storage; retain copies in legal folder and in escrow with dual-signature release controls.
- Post-signing verification: run signature verification on e-sign logs, verify PDF flattening, verify checksum matches recorded manifest hash, and log verification artifact to audit_chain.

### Quick technical steps to anchor signed file into manifest and escrow
- Flatten signed PDF → compute SHA-256:
  - sha256sum Signed_IP_Assignment_[Name].pdf
- Add entry to manifest/checksums.sha256:
  - sha256hex  path/to/Signed_IP_Assignment_[Name].pdf
- Sign updated checksums file with your signing key and include detached signature in manifest.
- Archive signed PDFs into escrow bundle and record their checksums and verification steps in escrow README.
