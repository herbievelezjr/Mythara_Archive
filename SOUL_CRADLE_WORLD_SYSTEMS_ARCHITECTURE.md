**Collective Reservoir (Team/Organization):**
- Team members pool benevolence

- Shared witnessing charges the collective reservoir

**Cultural Reservoir (Society/Civilization):**
- Society-wide accumulated wisdom, compassion, resilience
- Cultural practices that preserve benevolence (rituals, traditions, stories)
- Depletes during war, polarization, collapse
- Recharges through art, science, spirituality, connection

**Mathematical Model:**
```
dR/dt = recharge_rate - drain_rate + shared_inflow - shared_outflow

Where:
- recharge_rate = f(benevolence_episodes)  # witnessing paradoxes well
- drain_rate = f(collapse_risk, burnout)   # stress depletes reservoir
- shared_inflow = Σ(contributions from others)
- shared_outflow = Σ(support given to others)
```

**Key Insight:** Benevolence is **conserved** across scales — it flows like energy through a system.
