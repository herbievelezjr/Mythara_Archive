## 📘 Glossary of Terms — Mythara Engine

This glossary defines key technical, legal, and operational terms used throughout the Mythara Engine archive, escrow protocol, and licensing documentation.

| **Term** | **Definition** |
|---------|----------------|
| **Artifact** | Any reproducible output of Mythara Engine, including JSON files, fingerprints, manifests, and signed documents.  
| **Canonicalization** | The process of transforming data into a standardized, sorted, minified, and UTF-8 encoded format for reproducibility.  
| **Checksum** | A SHA-256 hash used to verify the integrity of an artifact. Stored in `manifest/checksums.sha256`.  
| **Detached Signature** | A cryptographic signature of the manifest, stored separately for legal custody and escrow verification.  
| **Deterministic Fingerprint** | A hash derived from RankedCandidate selection metadata, used to verify reproducibility across runs.  
| **Escrow Bundle** | A ZIP archive containing all reproducibility, legal, and accessibility artifacts prepared for deposit with an escrow agent.  
| **Fingerprint Block** | A JSON object containing selection metadata, locale, seed, and fingerprint hash. Used for acceptance verification.  
| **HSM/KMS** | Hardware Security Module / Key Management System used to sign manifests and control cryptographic keys.  
| **IP Assignment** | A signed legal document transferring intellectual property rights from contributors to the project owner.  
| **Locale Braille Map** | A JSON file mapping language-specific tokens to braille-compatible encodings for accessibility.  
| **Manifest** | A canonical list of all escrowed artifacts and their hashes. Used for verification and legal custody.  
| **Milestone Annex** | A contractual schedule of deliverables (e.g., pilot SOWs, escrow acceptance) tied to equity vesting or payment triggers.  
| **Reproducibility Harness** | A test suite that runs Mythara Engine multiple times with the same seed to confirm deterministic output.  
| **Remediation Log** | A record of security vulnerabilities identified and resolved during pen-testing.  
| **SOW (Statement of Work)** | A legal document defining scope, deliverables, and acceptance criteria for pilots or licenses.  
| **Verifier Command** | A CLI command used to validate fingerprint matches and manifest integrity.  
| **ZIP Protocol** | The standardized method for packaging, timestamping, and transferring escrow bundles. |
