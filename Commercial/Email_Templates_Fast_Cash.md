## When to Use $500 vs $2,500

**Use $500 when:**
- You need cash THIS WEEK
- Talking to smaller teams (startup, mid-market)
- Target seems price-sensitive
- You're in "volume mode" (10+ emails/day)
- First 2 weeks of outreach (build momentum)

**Use $2,500 when:**
- Talking to F500 or large enterprise
- Buyer is VP-level or C-suite
- They mention "budget approved"
