## Executive Summary

Mythara Engine is a deployed-ready inference and explainability layer that selects context-sensitive clause scaffolds, enforces safety fallbacks, and emits auditable explanations with an embedded, signed audit chain. Built for enterprise licensing and sovereign deployment, Mythara balances transparency for auditors with redaction of proprietary internals.

## Key Capabilities

- Clause orchestration: maps observables to clause_id outputs with harmonization scoring and deterministic constraints.  
- Safety-first rendering: shadow resolver and fallback clauses automatically engage on safety triggers.  
- Explainability: human-facing explanations with structured rationale, observables, and audit_chain (manifest_ref + prompt_hash + timestamp).  
- Accessibility: automatic braille and audio token generation; ≥99% delivery acceptance in validation.  
- Escrow & reproducibility: fully documented manifest, checksum, and PGP-signed artifacts for third-party reproduction.
